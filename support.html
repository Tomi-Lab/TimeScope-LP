Support & Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for installing, customizing, and using TimeScope – Visual Calendar on Windows.

Installation & Compatibility

What Windows versions are supported?

TimeScope is built for Windows 10 and Windows 11 (x64) and is distributed through Microsoft Store.

I launched TimeScope, where is the icon?

TimeScope runs in the Windows system tray. If it is inside the hidden tray overflow menu (▲), you can drag it onto the taskbar or enable it in Windows Settings → Personalization → Taskbar → Other system tray icons.

Calendar Display & Date-Range Selection

How many months can TimeScope display at once?

You can expand the calendar horizontally and vertically according to your available window and display space—there is no fixed six-month limit.

How do I measure the number of days between two dates?

Click and drag across any dates on the calendar. TimeScope highlights the selected period and displays the exact day count in a floating badge and the bottom summary bar, including across month boundaries.

How do I change zoom size, week start, or language?

Open the calendar menu to choose between Small, Medium, and Large zoom presets, switch Sunday or Monday week start, toggle ISO week numbers and relative days (-n / +n), or switch instantly among 7 supported languages with no restart.

TimeScope Pro & Purchases

What does TimeScope Pro include?

TimeScope Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase ($4.99 USD base price; local currency and taxes vary by region, no subscription) that unlocks Multi-band period comparison, Multi-monitor display selection, and Custom holidays & workdays.

How do I purchase or restore TimeScope Pro after reinstalling?

TimeScope Pro is linked to your Microsoft Store account via Microsoft Store in-app purchase inside the application. If you experience any issue purchasing or restoring your Pro entitlement after reinstalling, please contact support@gettimescope.com with your Microsoft Store order details.
